02 / THE FIVE LIGHTS

SOUL POTENTIAL

Potential measures the heights your soul may reach. It is a promise of possibility, not a guarantee of greatness.

Ⅰ
I · WICK — COMMON

A common flame. Modest promise, found in most souls; enough to illuminate an ordinary life.

Ⅱ
II · CANDLE — GIFTED

A gifted spark. With patience and discipline, its light can grow steady and useful.

Ⅲ
III · LANTERN — PRODIGY

A prodigy's light. Unusually bright, capable of guiding others and drawing notice.

Ⅳ
IV · BEACON — TITAN

A titan's flame. Rare and far-reaching; its bearer may change the fate of a battlefield.

Ⅴ
V · STAR — TRANSCENDENT

A transcendent light. Almost unheard of, with the potential to illuminate an age.

A brighter flame does not mean an easier path. Potential must still be earned.

03 / THE PRICE OF POWER

BLOOD RANK

Blood Rank measures present capability—not future promise. Every rise brings greater power, and a harsher price.

IMundane

No different in capability from an ordinary townsperson.

IIAttuned

You understand your magic. You read it, and it reads you back—but you remain inexperienced.

IIITrained

Trained and growing stronger. Your price intensifies; you can hold your own beyond the Gates.

IVStrengthened

Strengthened. You can fight effectively with magic and blade.

VVeteran

Where most students peak. A top killer among your squad, and a proven Reclaimer.

VICommander

Leadership status. You may handpick an expedition team and survive the deeper circles.

VIITyrant

A force of terrifying strength, able to survive depths where lesser squads are slaughtered.

VIIIAscendant

Too valuable to lose—or too vulnerable to corruption. Often offered a place among the faculty.

IXDemon King

Power equal to a Demon King. Few can stand against such a being and survive.

XGod

Godhood. The highest measure known; a power beyond mortal comprehension.

Blood Rank is not a promise of victory. Skill, experience, affinity, and circumstance still decide the fight.
